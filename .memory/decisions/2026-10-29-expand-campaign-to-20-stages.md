# Decisions: Expand Campaign to 20 Stages

- Date: 2026-10-29
- Status: Confirmed

## D01. Campaign size and progression
- **Chosen**: Make 20 total playable stages, numbered 1–20, unlocked in sequence.
- **Rationale**: The request is to expand the existing six-stage campaign into a longer linear adventure.

## D02. Regional structure and map navigation
- **Chosen**: Organize the campaign into four regions of five stages each. Show one region per map page, with that region's five stage nodes and previous/next region navigation.
- **Rationale**: This keeps the 20 choices readable and makes each group of five feel like a distinct chapter of the journey.

## D03. Existing levels and art
- **Chosen**: Reassign and revise existing stages 1–6 to fit the four-region campaign, then add the remaining stages to reach 20. Group and reuse the six existing backgrounds into four regional themes: alley and wall; rooftop and clocktower; factory; golden meadow.
- **Rationale**: This retains the project's authored environments while supporting four cohesive regions without requiring 20 unique backgrounds.

## D04. Boss placement
- **Chosen**: Keep the existing stage-6 cat boss as an early mid-boss. Add a new cat final boss for stage 20.
- **Rationale**: This preserves the existing boss encounter and gives the expanded campaign a new ending.

## D05. Stage mechanics
- **Chosen**: Continue combining and varying existing mechanics. Add one signature new gimmick per region, with the assistant designing each gimmick to fit its region.
- **Rationale**: New interactions give each region a clear identity while keeping the campaign anchored in the current physics system.

## D06. Chick evolution
- **Chosen**: Use one chick form per region and unlock the next form after clearing the previous region. The region ranges are stages 1–5, 6–10, 11–15, and 16–20, using the existing baby, breeze, fighter, and golden forms.
- **Rationale**: Evolution follows the four-region structure and gives each group of stages a clear progression reward.

## D07. Campaign progress persistence
- **Chosen**: Save completed stages, best star ratings, and challenge badges in browser storage so they survive refreshes and new sessions.
- **Rationale**: A 20-stage sequential campaign should resume where the player left off.

## D08. Stage content and difficulty
- **Chosen**: Provide complete playable data for all 20 stages, including title, theme, story dialogue, attack routes, spawn layouts, score target, and bonus challenge. Rework current stages as needed, use gradual difficulty progression, vary layouts and existing mechanics within each region, and introduce that region's new signature gimmick.
- **Rationale**: A campaign expansion should remain playable and narratively coherent from the first map through the final boss.
