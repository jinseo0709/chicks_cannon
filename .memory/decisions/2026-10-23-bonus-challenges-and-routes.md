# Bonus Challenges and Alternate Routes

- Date: 2026-10-23
- Status: Confirmed

## D01. Timer behavior
- **Chosen**: Use an optional bonus timer. Players can keep playing after it expires; there is no timeout failure.
- **Rationale**: Add urgency for players who want it without interrupting careful aiming.

## D02. Challenge schedule and reward
- **Chosen**: Give each stage one fixed timed challenge. A successful challenge adds score and earns a dedicated badge.
- **Rationale**: A clear goal makes the timer understandable and the badge gives a separate replay target.

## D03. Alternate route readability
- **Chosen**: Show two structurally distinct weak points on each stage and communicate the route cues clearly.
- **Rationale**: Players should understand that they can plan different attacks, while discovering the exact physics outcome themselves.

## D04. Challenge score and stars
- **Chosen**: Award challenge score on top of normal stage points. Keep the existing star requirements based on the normal stage score and remaining ammo.
- **Rationale**: The bonus challenge should feel rewarding without changing the established star thresholds.
